Confirmed blockers

CRITICAL ✓ 2/2 verified adversarial-ee

AW9523B reset sequencing violated on both telltale drivers (U11, U12)

RSTN (pin 23) is hard-tied to /+5V on both ICs. The datasheet mandates RSTN held low ~100 µs after VCC rises — "otherwise, the device may work incorrectly." Since the series resistors and DMOS driver were deliberately removed (KTD17), the POR-default-safe GPIO state is the only passive protection for all eight telltale LEDs, and that argument depends on the very POR logic this sequencing rule guards. A vehicle 5 V rail will brownout-cycle in exactly the way that breaks it.

RC delay or reset supervisor between /+5V and RSTN on both U11 and U12 — or a documented brownout-soak acceptance across many power cycles.

evidence

Netlist proof (kicad-cli sch export netlist): net /+5V contains both U11 pin 23 RSTN and U12 pin 23 RSTN as members of the same net as VCC — zero series impedance, not a separate RSTN net joined by a resistor.

AWINIC AW9523B datasheet (Mar 2024 V2.4), POWER ON section: "After power-up, about 100us delay is required before RSTN set to high" (Figure 6 shows RSTN as a distinct signal held low after VBAT establishes). Internal R_RSTN pull-down is 100 kΩ typ — the chip's own default is low; the hard tie overrides it. AD-strapping is separately verified POR-safe (both ICs land on Table 1's row driving P1_4–P1_7 HIGH = zero LED differential at POR) — but only if POR completes correctly.

HIGH ✓ 2/2 verified adversarial-ee

No overcurrent protection anywhere on the board

The full BOM contains no fuse, PTC, or polyfuse of any kind. The barrel jack (DC1) feeds straight through the ideal-diode path (Q2 + U6 LM74700 — reverse polarity: covered) with no current-limiting element. A stale schematic note says the intended supply is "dedicated 5V/15A" — if that's real, a board fault is destructive, not just inconvenient.

Resettable PTC sized to actual load, between DC1 and Q2/U6. Confirm or delete the "5V/15A" annotation while there.

evidence

fab_audit/bom.csv (51 lines): only L1 (ferrite bead) and L2 (buck inductor) — no fuse part class. Netlist confirms DC1 → Q2 (AO4406A) gated by U6 onto /+5V_BARREL with no current-sense or limit element. Stale annotation at .kicad_sch line ~14897.

HIGH ✓ 2/2 verified fab-readiness

U2 QSPI flash: 4 units of live JLCPCB stock

W25Q512JVEIQ (LCSC C7389628, WSON-8, Extended part, qty 1 required) showed stock = 4 at review time. Other package variants of the same MPN: 3, 0, and 0. One other customer's order between quote and submission stalls the whole PCBA run. Related: U1 (STM32H755ZIT6, C730212) sits at 37 — survivable, but thin.

Re-verify live stock at the moment of order; qualify a pin-compatible alternative (different W25Q512JV package/reel) beforehand. Re-check U1 too.

evidence

Live jlc_stock_check query, verified 2026-07-31: C7389628 stock=4; C2986165 stock=3; C3034691 and C2895611 both stock=0.

HIGH ✓ 2/2 verified dfa

AW9523B footprint has no courtyard — every courtyard check silently passed U11/U12 by testing nothing

The AW9523BTQR footprint contains zero F.CrtYd/B.CrtYd geometry (confirmed by reading both placed instances end-to-end and grepping the whole file). Courtyard-overlap tooling reports "clean" for these two ICs because there is nothing to test — a hard blind spot on exactly the two parts with the highest field-diagnosis risk. Manual reconstruction puts U12's body within ~0.4–0.55 mm of LED6's body, never checked by any tool.

Add an F.CrtYd rectangle (~4.3 × 4.3 mm) to the AW9523BTQR footprint in JLCImport.pretty, re-run the courtyard check, and eyeball U12 vs LED6 in the 2D view.

HIGH ✓ 2/2 verified dfa

LED4, LED6, LED7 have no polarity mark on printed silkscreen

Three of the eight telltale LEDs carry their polarity triangle only on F.Fab — which is never printed. Actual silkscreen is two symmetric lines: nothing distinguishes anode from cathode on the assembled board. A reversed telltale LED fails as wrong indicator lit — the hardest field symptom to trace back to a placement error. The other five LED footprints on this board all carry a proper silk mark.

Add a visible cathode mark on F.SilkS to the HL-A-3528H203BC and HL-A-3528S35FC footprints, matching the pattern the other LED footprints already use.

evidence

Verified two ways: direct read of both board instances (2 plain symmetric fp_line on F.SilkS each), and F.SilkS element counts across the master JLCImport.pretty library — affected footprints: 3 (2 lines + hidden ref text); unaffected LED footprints: 6–19 elements including asymmetric marks.

HIGH ✓ 2/2 verified dfa

Solid 100% paste apertures under all three exposed-pad parts (U11, U12, U2)

Both AW9523B QFN-24s (EP 2.8 × 2.8 mm) and the W25Q512 WSON-8 (EP 5.2 × 4.65 mm) print a single unwindowed paste deposit covering the entire exposed pad. Whole-board grep: zero solder_paste_margin anywhere. A solid deposit that size traps flux outgassing during reflow — the standard cause of voiding, part float, and tombstoning under EPs (IPC-7093 calls for ~50–80% windowed coverage).

Segment the EP paste apertures to ~50–70% checkerboard coverage in both footprints before the stencil is ordered.

What's genuinely solid

The copper is in the best state this board has been in — and that's double-verified, not self-reported:
  • 0 DRC violations at all severities, confirmed by two independent tool paths. This branch resolved all 150 baseline violations, identity-diffed — nothing was traded for a new violation.
  • Every multi-pad net fully connected, including the historically fragile /+5V telltale daisy-chain (re-derived via standalone pcbnew connectivity graph, not regex).
  • Zone topology correct: 3× /GND filled, 1× /+5V, keepouts netless by definition.
  • All measured geometry inside every JLCPCB capability limit that could be live-verified; staged rules match JLC's published 4-layer/1 oz numbers.
  • Reverse-polarity and ESD protection verified sound (LM74700 ideal-diode ORing, USBLC6-2SC6, TJA1051's rated ±8 kV on the CAN terminals).

Caveats on the review itself

Two kicad_interface.py processes (PIDs 8264, 33472) were observed running concurrently. One may be a stale orphan — confirm before killing either; they may be shared across agent sessions like the EasyEDA bridge.

Method

Six specialist review lenses ran in parallel against the full current board state (not the branch diff): fab-readiness / sourcing, DRC & routing topology, adversarial electrical engineering, complexity reduction, DFM fab-capability, and DFA assembly. 637 tool calls across 19 agents; live JLCPCB stock and capability lookups; DRC re-run from scratch both via the KiCad MCP and via kicad_verify.py under KiCad's own interpreter.

Every CRITICAL/HIGH finding was then handed to two independent verification agents instructed to refute it using their own tool access. All six findings survived 12/12 votes. MEDIUM/LOW findings passed through unverified and are labeled as such.
